noncommercial use at no cost. Commercial use requires a separate paid license

## Free under the noncommercial license

The license text is authoritative, but in practice this covers:

- Personal use, study, experimentation, hobby projects, and evaluation
- Charitable organizations and nonprofits
- Educational institutions, including teaching and academic research
- Public research organizations
- Public safety and health organizations
- Environmental protection organizations
- Government institutions, including state, county, and municipal GIS departments

The license grants this "regardless of the source of funding or obligations
resulting from the funding," so a grant-funded or contract-funded project at one
of those organizations is still covered.

## Requires a commercial license

If you are a for-profit business, the short test is whether the work is
primarily intended for or directed toward commercial advantage or monetary
compensation. That includes:

- Consultancies and engineering, planning, or environmental firms using the
  bridge on billable client work or on deliverables sold to a client
- Using it to produce maps, analysis, or data products you sell or license
- Embedding it in, or shipping it alongside, a commercial product or service
- Internal use by a for-profit company in the ordinary course of business
- Offering it as a hosted or managed service

Evaluation is free. You do not need a license to install it, run it against a
test project, or decide whether it is worth adopting. You need one before it
touches paid work.

## Getting a commercial license

Terms are scoped per engagement rather than sold off a price list, because
deployments differ in seat count, support expectations, and how much
configuration and integration work is involved.

To start a conversation, open an issue on this repository. If you reached this
project through a referral or a contact card, use the details you already have.
Useful context to include:

- What your organization does, and roughly how many analysts would use it
- Which ArcGIS Pro version and deployment model you are on
- Whether you need support, custom tools or workflows, or help with integration
- Any procurement or data-handling constraints that would shape the agreement
